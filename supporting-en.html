01 / BEACON & MIT

The Beacon and MIT

Beacon Admissions Chair

Theodore

  • Age 20
  • Male
  • 187cm
  • ENTJ
  • A Type 1 leopard beastfolk and the fourth-generation sole son of the Hales, an oil dynasty.
  • A third-year chemical engineering student at MIT who fences épée.
    He oversees Beacon admissions and went to the same elite private high school as Ian Shen.
  • Handsome, with short platinum-blond hair and blue-gray eyes.
    He has a muscular build, leopard ears, and a leopard tail.

Beacon Social Committee

Grant

  • Age 21
  • Male
  • 190cm
  • ESTP
  • A Type 2 spotted hyena beastfolk and the third son of the Pikes, a food industry dynasty.
  • A third-year management science student at MIT.
    He serves on the Beacon's social committee and oversees new members at his fraternity.
  • He speaks for Theodore and has a knack for making pointed remarks sound like jokes.
  • He has a muscular build, short brown hair, and golden eyes.

Beacon Treasurer

Walter

  • Age 21
  • Male
  • 189cm
  • ISTJ
  • A Type 2 Doberman beastfolk and the eldest son of the Whitlocks, a semiconductor dynasty.
  • A fourth-year materials science and engineering student at MIT.
    As the Beacon's treasurer, he manages the Panda Wager's accumulated pot.
  • He has short black hair and dark eyes.

Middle Linebacker

Dariard

  • Age 21
  • Male
  • 193cm
  • ENFJ
  • A Type 2 orca beastfolk and the second son of the Asters, a shipping dynasty.
  • A fourth-year mechanical engineering student at MIT and a full Beacon member.
    He plays middle linebacker on the football team and trains with the goal of reaching the NFL.
  • He has a muscular build, black hair, white eyes, and an orca tail.

Full Beacon Member

Sloane

  • Age 20
  • Female
  • 173cm
  • ESFP
  • A Type 1 coyote beastfolk and the youngest daughter of the Whitakers, a media dynasty.
  • A third-year comparative media studies student at MIT and a full Beacon member.
    Known around campus for her looks, she's used to being the center of attention.
  • She has a curvy build, blonde hair, and green eyes.

Second in Her MIT Economics Cohort

Rin

  • Age 21
  • Female
  • 168cm
  • ENFJ
  • Her full name is Min Se-rin.
    A Type 1 otter beastfolk and the eldest daughter of the owner of a midsize Korean distribution company.
  • An international student from South Korea, she ranks second among MIT's third-year economics students.
    She hopes to join the Beacon.
  • She has a slender build, a gray hime cut, and dark eyes.

02 / FAMILY

Ian Shen's family

Chairman of Zhiheng Group

Shen Jiming 沈既明

  • Age 50
  • Male
  • 181cm
  • ESTJ
  • A panda beastfolk, chairman of Zhiheng Group, and Ian Shen's father.
  • He works diligently and pays close attention to details.
    He's much more easygoing around his family.
  • He has a solid build, short white hair, dark eyes, and a beard.

Ian Shen's Mother

Zhang Zihan 张梓涵

  • Age 43
  • Female
  • 172cm
  • ENFP
  • A brown bear beastfolk who grew up as the cherished daughter of a senior Party official.
    She fell for Shen Jiming at twenty and made the first move. They went on to marry.
  • She looks after the people around her. Her enthusiasm sometimes makes her scatterbrained or leads her to get involved in other people's affairs.
    She often texts her son Ian to check in.
  • She has a slim build, a youthful face, long brown hair, and dark eyes.
    She has brown bear ears and a brown bear tail.
