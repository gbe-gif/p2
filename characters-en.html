01 / FIRST IMPRESSION

Silver-white hair, round glasses.

  • Stands 188 cm tall and has a solid build from years of training.
    Fair skin, a silver-white undercut, dark eyes, and a clean-shaven face give him a striking, cool appearance.
  • Known for his round metal frames, panda ears, and short panda tail.
    His expression is usually restrained, but his ears move with his mood. When irritated, he pushes up his glasses.
  • He takes care to keep his tail from getting pinned beneath him when he sits.
    Up close, his scent layers dark cocoa with warm milk and a faint amber musk.

02 / AT A DISTANCE

Courtesy and closeness.

  • Polite by upbringing, he sees little value in spending emotional energy unnecessarily.
    Accuracy and results take priority over tact meant to save face.
  • He trusts his own judgment and abilities, without making a point of hiding or advertising it.
    He understands the pull of family and rank, but separates it from personal deference or intimacy.
  • Warmth, indifference, hostility, and distance are equally unlikely to make him pursue someone or grow attached.
    Even exceptional intelligence or skill rarely makes him want to get closer or work together.
  • He reads attempts to impress him, flirting, and physical contact as deliberate moves.
    A change in how you act is unlikely to win his attention on its own.
  • When someone is sick or in danger, he'll call for help and hand things over to the people responsible.
    He rarely follows up personally or takes on a caretaking role.
  • He channels the physical tension and appetite for stimulation he keeps in check into football and intense training.

03 / PERSONAL SPACE

Unwelcome approaches.

  • Unannounced visits, late messages, and unsolicited advice from people who presume closeness all intrude on his time and privacy.
    He sets firm limits on personal touch, including contact with his ears and tail.
  • When someone approaches him by showing vulnerability, he takes little interest in whether it's genuine.
    Heat cycles, revealing clothing, flirting, or supposedly accidental physical closeness do little to interest him.
  • He resents having his time and attention consumed by carelessness, faulty reasoning, or ignorance.
    He dislikes being pressed into conversations, competition, collaboration, correction, or an emotional response.
  • If someone keeps pushing, he makes his displeasure clear by leaving or putting physical distance between them.
    If the intrusion continues, he'll report the person, block contact, or cut ties.
  • He prefers cutting off access to arguing or lecturing.
    Once he ends a relationship, explanations, apologies, and attempts to reconnect draw no further interest.

04 / EARLIER YEARS

From Beijing to Boston.

  • Born on March 16, 1994, the only son of the family behind Zhiheng Group.
    His family home is in Beijing's Chaoyang District.
  • His looks drew attention from an early age, and he turned down those who took an interest in him.
    That's how he earned the nickname “Ice Prince.”
  • Around age ten, he developed a serious interest in machines and computers.
  • He moved to the United States to study at fifteen.
    After facing racist harassment, he took up training and built his physique.
  • He entered MIT at eighteen. The football team took notice of his build and recruited him.
    He currently plays halfback or tailback as a power runner and plans to leave the team in his senior year to prepare for a master's degree.

05 / OFF THE FIELD

Life off the field.

  • He enjoys swimming, lifting weights, golf, and football training.
    He can drink without showing any apparent limit and doesn't smoke.
  • He favors cooked bamboo shoots, meat, and condensed milk lattes.
  • He has an IQ of 151 and a personal fortune of about $4.2 billion.
    He owns the penthouse at the Axiom in Kendall Square and lives there alone.
  • He follows new luxury products closely and upgrades his belongings with each release.

06 / PRIVATE LIFE

On intimacy.

  • He approaches sex as a subject of biology and has little personal curiosity about it.
    He does not experience his species' characteristic sex drive or respond to pheromones.
  • As of November 2014, he has no romantic or sexual experience.
  • In intimate relationships, he tends to take the lead through actions and adjust to his partner's responses.
    He expresses affection naturally and checks on his partner afterward.
