THE WORLD

A familiar world,
a different order.

A modern world shared by humans and beastfolk.
Sometimes who you know matters more than what you are.

Humans and beastfolk live at a ratio of about one to nine. Anti-discrimination laws protect different species, and public facilities and everyday goods accommodate their needs.

Beastfolk are distinct from animals. When parents are of different species, their child has an equal chance of inheriting either parent's species.

All women are Type 1. Around 90% of men are Type 2 and can get a partner pregnant. The remaining men are Type 1 and can carry a pregnancy. Depending on their species, Type 1 people may experience heat cycles.

01 — THE SOCIETY

“People who know the same names
and walk through the same doors.”

A private, invitation-only collegiate social network backed by Boston's old-money families. Its connections span MIT, Harvard, other elite schools, and several fraternities and sororities.

Membership is open to graduates of elite private schools or candidates recommended by an existing member. There are no species or racial restrictions, though beastfolk of carnivorous species from old American fortunes dominate its inner circle.

Charity dinners, academic sponsorships, internships, and alumni events give members reasons to meet. Family donations, businesses, private clubs, and vacation homes provide venues and opportunities.

The Beacon holds no formal authority over fraternities. Its members nevertheless wield influence as officers, donors, and party hosts.

LIFE ON CAMPUS

One campus,
different perspectives.

Across the Charles River, a campus full of possibilities.
Some lines there are harder to see.

Humans and beastfolk study in the same departments, live in the same dorms, and play on the same teams at MIT. The institute prohibits discrimination by species.

Rare species still draw curiosity and face stereotypes and subtle everyday discrimination.

The Beacon occasionally issues difficult challenges. Anyone who succeeds can be admitted without meeting the usual membership requirements.

THE PANDA WAGER / SINCE 2010

THE MOST ELUSIVE PRIZE

The Panda
Wager.

An old bet. A winter that isn't over yet.

Theodore first proposed the wager in 2010, back in private school. It followed the group to college and remains one of the MIT Beacon's oldest and toughest unsolved challenges.

The wager opens every November and ends at midnight on December 24. The winner is the first person to receive a kiss initiated by Ian Shen.

Fraternities, sororities, and Beacon members at other schools also take part. The unclaimed pot has grown to a staggering sum, though some full members oppose the wager and sit it out.

You're a Type 1 student at MIT, and you're in this year's wager. Your reasons for joining are up to you.

01 / THE FAMILY

Zhiheng Group 知衡集团

Zhiheng Group

One of China's wealthiest established business families, with global interests in finance, energy, technology, and logistics.

The family has traditionally been led by panda beastfolk. It is expanding its technology business and investments.

02 / THE RESIDENCE

The Axiom

Kendall Square · Boston area

An upscale mixed-use condominium completed in 2013. Its restrained glass, stone, and black metal exterior stands about seven minutes on foot from MIT's East Campus.

The building includes a luxury grocery store on the ground floor, round-the-clock concierge service and security, underground parking, a residents' gym, and a 25-meter indoor pool. Guests need a resident's approval to use the residential elevators.

03 / THE PENTHOUSE

Above the city

The Axiom · Floors 23–24

Ian Shen lives alone in the two-story penthouse, with regular visits from a housekeeper. The space overlooks the Charles River and the city skyline.

Pale gray stone, dark walnut, and matte black define the minimalist interior, which is fitted with high-end appliances. Downstairs, floor-to-ceiling windows, a tail-friendly sofa, and a premium sound system furnish the living room. The floor also has a dining room, open kitchen, shared bathroom, and two guest rooms.

Upstairs, the primary bedroom has a king bed and sculptural lighting, alongside a dressing room and large bathroom. This level also houses a study, server room, weight room, and terrace.
